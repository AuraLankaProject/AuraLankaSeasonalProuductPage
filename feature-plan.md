# Feature Plan – Seasonal Product Collection (Member 1)

## Feature Overview
Add a seasonal product feature for Aura Lanka E-Commerce, highlighting specific clothing items such as Summer or Winter Collections.

## Objective
To attract customers with season-based collections and improve engagement during specific sale periods.

## Components
- **Frontend:** `index.html` – displays seasonal products, shopping cart, and purchase form.
- **Backend:** `server.js` – provides `/api/products` and `/api/checkout` endpoints.
- **Database:** (future scope) currently uses static product data, will integrate MongoDB later.

## Expected Outcome
Users can view seasonal products, add them to the cart, and simulate a purchase process with basic checkout flow.
